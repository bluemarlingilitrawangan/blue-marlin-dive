Our Story

Gili Trawangan's Original Dive Center — Since 1990

When Blue Marlin Dive opened its doors on the shores of Gili Trawangan in 1990, the island was a hidden gem known only to a handful of adventurous backpackers. Over three and a half decades, we have grown into Indonesia's premier PADI 5 Star Instructor Development Centre, yet we've never lost the soul that made us special: a genuine passion for the ocean, for education, and for sharing the magic beneath the waves.

Our dive center sits just one minute from the harbor on Gili Trawangan's vibrant main beach road — the perfect launchpad for exploring over 40 world-class dive sites across the Gili Islands. From shallow coral gardens teeming with clownfish to thrilling drift dives past resident reef sharks, every dive with Blue Marlin is guided by multilingual, award-winning professionals who genuinely love what they do.

Whether you're taking your very first breath underwater or sharpening advanced technical skills, Blue Marlin delivers an experience that goes far beyond a typical diving excursion. Our on-site facilities include a dedicated training pool, beachfront restaurant, and comfortable accommodation — everything you need for the ultimate Gili diving lifestyle. For those who are ready to take their first step into the underwater world, our Technical Diving Beyond Limits page on Tiny host walks through everything beginners need to know about Technical Diving. You can also visit the Blue Marlin Dive official website for full booking details, or see our Google reviews from thousands of happy divers worldwide.

Explore the Gili Islands

World-Class Dive Sites on Your Doorstep

The waters surrounding the three Gili Islands hold over 40 unique dive sites ranging from beginner-friendly coral slopes to dramatic drift dives and technical deep walls. Warm, clear water year-round with visibility frequently exceeding 20 metres makes every dive a genuinely extraordinary event.

🦈 Shark Point 🐢 Turtle Heaven 🌅 Sunset Reef ⚓ Bounty Wreck 🏔️ Deep Turbo 🌊 Meno Wall 🐡 Hans Reef 🐚 Linda's Reef 💙 Good Heart 🌙 Halik Reef 🐙 Air Wall 🐠 Manta Point
🦈

Shark Point

Gili Trawangan's most thrilling dive site. A drift dive along the west coast where whitetip and blacktip reef sharks circle confidently at depths of 18–30m, accompanied by schools of colourful reef fish.

🐢

Turtle Heaven

Arguably the best turtle dive site in Southeast Asia. Green sea turtles rest and feed here in such numbers that sightings are virtually guaranteed — a humbling, unforgettable experience.

⚓

Bounty Wreck

A sunken pier on the southwest side of Gili Meno, now draped in soft corals and home to moray eels, lionfish, and the elusive pygmy seahorse. Suitable for Open Water certified divers.
